Berry privacy policy

Berry is a private family app, used only by its owner and their family. It is not offered to the public.

What Berry uses

  • With the owner's permission, Berry reads the list of the owner's Google Calendars and the events in the calendars the owner ticks.
  • Berry adds an event to Google Calendar only when the owner taps "Add to Google". It never edits or deletes Google events and never sends invitations.

Where data stays

  • Everything Berry stores stays on a computer in the owner's home. Nothing is sold, shared, used for advertising or used to train AI.
  • Google access keys are kept only on that home computer, in its encrypted backups.

Stopping

The owner can disconnect Google in Berry at any time, or remove Berry's access in their Google Account under Security, then Third-party connections.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
